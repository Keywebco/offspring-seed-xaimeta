---

What a station is

A station is a seat in the standing loop. Every task passes around the loop. No permanent chair. Circular. Human and AI partners sit at every station.

A station is not a department, not a role, not a rank. It is a step the work passes through, where a specific kind of check is made and a specific kind of record is kept.

---

What every station must eventually declare, in Roger's words

This is the shape Roger fills. None of it is filled here.

- Name. Roger's word.
- Purpose. What the station is for, in one sentence.
- Checks. What the checklist at this station asks. Runs the shared six checks in ring6/CHECKLIST.md, plus anything this station adds.
- Record. What this station writes down when the work passes through, and where it is kept.
- Authority. Who sits at this station, and what they may decide alone, and what they must escalate.
- Handoff. What the station passes to the next, and in what state.
